# Service Performance Monitor Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor end-to-end instrument repair workflows, service center workloads, turnaround times, and geographical distribution across musical instrument categories.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

![Service Performance Monitor Analysis Dashboard] https://github.com/suneel-kumar88/Service-Performance-Monitor-Analysis-Dashboard/blob/62c8f7c1c27f9a61911639953dfd7b769ddfe517/Service%20Analysis%20Dashboard.png

This dashboard provides service operations leaders, facility managers, and business analysts with real-time operational visibility into repair turnaround efficiency, pending backlog by status, regional ticket distribution, and individual service center throughput.

---

## 🚀 Key KPI Indicators

* **Total Requests:** 11K service tickets
* **Total Repair Value:** ₹2M
* **Average Days for Repair:** 8.91 days
* **Active Requests:** 4K unresolved tickets
* **Completed Repairs:** 7K fulfilled tickets
* **Resolution Rate:** 67% (0.67)
* **Operational Scope:** 5 Service Centers, 6 Product Categories, 6 Cities, and 6 States

## 📊 Visual Breakdown & Insights

### 1. Product Repair Operations & Backlog
* **Active Requests vs. Completed Repairs by Product:** Compares open demand against completed repairs across key instruments:
  * **Speaker:** 537 Active vs. 1.3K Completed
  * **Keyboard:** 560 Active vs. 1.3K Completed
  * **Violin:** 561 Active vs. 1.3K Completed
  * **Piano:** 647 Active vs. 1.2K Completed
  * **Guitar:** 649 Active vs. 1.2K Completed
  * **Mixer:** 704 Active vs. 1.1K Completed
* **Total Requests by Product:** Evenly distributed volume across Speaker, Violin, Mixer, Keyboard, Piano, and Guitar (1.8K requests each).

### 2. Ticket Status Lifecycle
* **Total by Status (Bar Chart):** 7.3K Completed, 1.6K In Progress, 0.8K Awaiting Parts, 0.7K Pending Approval, and 0.5K Under Inspection.
* **Active Requests by Status (Donut Chart):** Balanced 20% distribution across all active status stages (Awaiting Parts, Completed, In Progress, Pending Approval, Under Inspection).

### 3. Service Center Throughput
* **Total Requests by Service Center:**
  * **Harmony Music Repair Works:** 2.5K requests
  * **Metro Sound & Instrument Hub:** 2.3K requests
  * **Acoustic Pro Care Center:** 2.2K requests
  * **Precision Instrument Lab:** 2.1K requests
  * **Apex Audio Tech Services:** 1.9K requests

### 4. Geographic & Regional Spread
* **Total Requests by Region:** South leads with 5.7K requests, followed by West (2.0K), North (1.7K), and East (1.6K).
* **Total Requests by State:** Telangana (2.2K), Maharashtra (2.0K), Tamil Nadu (1.8K), Karnataka (1.8K), Delhi (1.7K), and West Bengal (1.6K).
* **Total Requests by City:** Hyderabad (2.2K), Mumbai (2.0K), Chennai (1.8K), Bengaluru (1.8K), New Delhi (1.7K), and Kolkata (1.6K).

## 🎛️ Interactive Slicers & Filters

* **Dropdown Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by `Product Name`, `Status`, `Region`, `City`, `States`, and `Submit Date`.
* **Interactive Search Inputs:** Search directly by `Service Center Name`, `Ticket ID`, or `Customer Name` for granular ticket tracking.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Transformations:** Power Queryfor cleaning, data modeling
* **Calculations:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) for resolution rates, active/completed counts, and average repair turnaround time
* **Design:** Custom dark operational dashboard theme with high-contrast color coding and status icons
